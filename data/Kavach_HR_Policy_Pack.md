# About this pack

This pack summarises the HR policies that apply to all employees of Kavach Insurtech Pvt. Ltd. ("Kavach"). Where this pack is silent, employees should contact their HR Business Partner (HRBP).

**Bands at Kavach**

| Band | Levels | Typical roles |
|---|---|---|
| A (Associate) | A1, A2, A3 | Executives, Senior Executives, Relationship Managers |
| M (Manager) | M1, M2 | Managers, Senior Managers |
| S (Senior Leadership) | S1, S2 | Associate Directors, Directors |
| Intern | — | Interns (fixed-term, stipend-based) |

**Role types:** Corporate, Field Sales, Intern.

**Probation:** All new employees (except interns) are on probation for the first **6 months** from their date of joining. The employee's probation status is shown in the HRMS ("PeopleHub").

**City tiers (used in this pack)**

- **Tier 1:** Mumbai, Delhi NCR (Delhi, Gurugram, Noida), Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune
- **Tier 2/3:** all other cities

# 1. Leave Policy

**1.1 Leave year.** The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

**1.2 Earned Leave (EL).** EL is credited monthly in equal parts.

| Band | EL per year | Maximum carry-forward to next year |
|---|---|---|
| A | 18 days | 30 days |
| M | 21 days | 30 days |
| S | 24 days | 45 days |

EL above the carry-forward limit lapses on 31 December. EL can be encashed only at the time of exit, up to a maximum of 30 days.

**1.3 Casual and Sick Leave (CL/SL).** All bands get **12 days of CL/SL combined** per year. CL/SL cannot be carried forward or encashed. Sick leave of **more than 2 consecutive days** requires a medical certificate uploaded in PeopleHub.

**1.4 Leave during probation.** Employees on probation accrue EL from their date of joining but can **use EL only after confirmation**. In an emergency, EL may be used during probation with approval from both the reporting manager and the HRBP. CL/SL can be used during probation.

**1.5 Interns.** Interns get **1 day of leave per completed month** of internship. It cannot be carried forward or encashed. No other leave type applies to interns.

**1.6 Maternity Leave.** 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children; 12 weeks from the third child onwards.

**1.7 Paternity Leave.** 5 working days, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.

**1.8 Bereavement Leave.** 3 working days on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, parent-in-law).

**1.9 How to apply.** Apply in PeopleHub: *Leave > Apply Leave*. The reporting manager should act within 3 working days; if not, the request is escalated to the HRBP.

# 2. Attendance and Work From Home

**2.1 Working hours.** Corporate offices: 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Field Sales employees mark attendance using the geo-tagged punch in the PeopleHub mobile app.

**2.2 Late marks.** A punch-in after **9:45 AM** is a late mark. Up to **3 late marks per calendar month** are allowed. The **4th and every later late mark** in the same month results in a **half-day deduction** from leave (EL first; if no EL balance, loss of pay).

**2.3 Missed punch / regularisation.** A missed punch must be regularised in PeopleHub within **5 calendar days** of the date. A maximum of **4 regularisations per month** is allowed.

**2.4 Work From Home (WFH).**

| Band | WFH allowed (Corporate role type) |
|---|---|
| A | Up to 2 days per month, with manager approval |
| M | Up to 4 days per month, with manager approval |
| S | Flexible, as agreed with the reporting manager |

**Field Sales employees of any band are not eligible for WFH.**

# 3. Travel and Reimbursement

**3.1 Hotel limit per night**

| Band | Tier 1 city | Tier 2/3 city |
|---|---|---|
| A | ₹3,500 | ₹2,500 |
| M | ₹5,500 | ₹4,000 |
| S | ₹8,000 | ₹6,000 |

**3.2 Daily allowance (food and incidentals) per day of travel:** A ₹600 · M ₹900 · S ₹1,200.

**3.3 Mode of travel**

- **Band A:** Train (3AC) for journeys up to 500 km; economy flight allowed only for journeys above 500 km.
- **Band M:** Economy flight for any distance.
- **Band S:** Economy flight; premium economy allowed if the flight is longer than 4 hours.

**3.4 Local conveyance (own vehicle):** car ₹12 per km, two-wheeler ₹5 per km.

**3.5 Submitting claims.** Claims with bills must be submitted in PeopleHub (*Expenses > New Claim*) within **30 days** of the trip ending. Claims submitted between 31 and 60 days need HRBP approval. **Claims submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed.**

# 4. Code of Conduct and Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)

**4.1** Kavach has zero tolerance for sexual harassment at the workplace, including at client sites, offsites and on digital channels.

**4.2 Internal Committee (IC).** Complaints are handled by the Internal Committee constituted under the POSH Act, 2013. A complaint can be raised by email to **ic@kavach.example** or in writing to any IC member.

**4.3 Timeline.** A complaint should be made within **3 months** of the incident (or of the last incident, in case of a series). The IC may extend this by up to 3 more months for valid reasons.

**4.4 Confidentiality and no retaliation.** The identity of the complainant, respondent and witnesses is kept confidential. Retaliation against anyone who raises a complaint in good faith is a disciplinary offence.

**4.5** Employees may also reach out to their HRBP for support in raising a complaint.

# 5. Separation (Exit)

**5.1 Notice period**

| Status / Band | Notice period |
|---|---|
| On probation (any band) | 15 days |
| Confirmed — Band A | 30 days |
| Confirmed — Band M | 60 days |
| Confirmed — Band S | 90 days |
| Intern | 7 days |

**5.2 How to resign.** Submit the resignation in PeopleHub: *Separation > Resign*. The reporting manager and HRBP are notified automatically.

**5.3 Notice buy-out.** The notice period may be shortened by paying the shortfall (calculated on basic salary), subject to HRBP approval.

**5.4 Full and Final settlement.** F&F is processed within **45 days** of the last working day.
